# Contributing

Contributions are welcome if they strengthen legal-source grounding, citation
hygiene, public connectors, synthetic evals or deterministic utilities.

## Good contributions

- official-source connector adapters;
- schemas and tests;
- synthetic verification cases;
- documentation for safe legal AI workflows;
- bug fixes to public utilities.

## Not accepted

- private client documents;
- copied proprietary prompts;
- unofficial legal advice held out as final;
- scraped databases whose terms prohibit redistribution;
- credentials, tokens or private endpoints;
- claims of verified law without official-source support.

## Style

Small, auditable PRs are preferred.
