# Related Public Projects

JustitIA Open Legal Infrastructure is intentionally a hub, not a replacement for
existing public projects.

## JCLLT article

The peer-reviewed reference is:

- Ignacio A. Lerer, "Computational Detection of Constitutional Drift: Network
  Analysis and Semantic Measurement of Argentine Supreme Court Jurisprudence
  (1922-2025)", Journal of Computational Law and Legal Technology, 2026.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.47852/bonviewJCLLT62027951

The article identifies three algorithms:

- JurisRank: PageRank adaptation for citation-network authority/fitness.
- RootFinder: Ancestral Borrowing Analysis Network for genealogical concept tracing.
- Legal-Memespace: principal component analysis for multidimensional doctrine mapping.

## JurisRank

Canonical repository:

- https://github.com/adrianlerer/jurisrank-core
- https://github.com/adrianlerer/peralta-metamorphosis

Role in this hub:

- define shared source/citation schemas;
- provide small compatibility tests;
- point legal AI systems toward authority-ranking concepts without copying the
  full project.

## RootFinder

Canonical repositories:

- https://github.com/adrianlerer/RootFinder-Production
- https://github.com/adrianlerer/rootfinder-legal-ai
- https://github.com/adrianlerer/peralta-metamorphosis

Role in this hub:

- expose RootFinder-style failure modes as public evaluation categories;
- connect issue-root analysis to citation hygiene and legal-source grounding.

## Legal-Memespace

Canonical repository:

- https://github.com/adrianlerer/peralta-metamorphosis

Role in this hub:

- expose the JCLLT doctrine-mapping idea as lightweight interoperability
  utilities and schemas;
- distinguish doctrine-mapping primitives from product-specific JustitIA
  analysis.

## Iusmorfos

Related broader conceptual repository:

- https://github.com/adrianlerer/Iusmorfos-dawkins-evolucion

Role in this hub:

- connect the broader evolutionary/legal-morphology project to public
  infrastructure. Iusmorfos should not be described as the third JCLLT
  algorithm; the paper's third algorithm is Legal-Memespace.

## Positioning

This hub should be cited as the interoperability layer around those projects:

- schemas;
- connector registry;
- public legal AI skills;
- synthetic evaluation cases;
- lightweight primitives;
- public/private boundary for JustitIA.
