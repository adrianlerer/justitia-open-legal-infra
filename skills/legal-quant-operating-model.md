# Skill: Legal Quant Operating Model

Public, sanitized operating model for lawyers who turn legal judgment,
documents, workflows, evals, and institutional memory into inspectable systems.

## Purpose

When the legal-AI application layer becomes easy to replicate, durable value
moves to judgment, trust, proprietary intelligence, workflow integration,
verification, and distribution. This skill helps separate real legal-system
capability from generic AI-wrapper usage.

## Use when

- evaluating whether a legal-AI workflow is substantive or merely a wrapper;
- turning a repeated legal task into a workflow or internal tool;
- designing advanced legal-AI features for a law firm, legal department, or
  compliance team;
- assessing proof-of-work from lawyers who claim AI-native capability;
- deciding what should stay public infrastructure and what should remain
  proprietary firm/client intelligence.

## Capability layers

1. **Prompting and evals**
   - Structured prompts, source requirements, known-answer tests, and failure
     cases.
2. **Workflow building**
   - Multi-step legal workflows with defined inputs, outputs, gates, and review
     states.
3. **Document systems**
   - Contracts, laws, judgments, pleadings, policies, and diligence packs modeled
     as dependency systems rather than loose paragraphs.
4. **Agent orchestration**
   - Specialized roles for research, drafting, citation checking, adversarial
     review, plain-language review, formatting, and governance.
5. **Proprietary intelligence**
   - Firm/client memory, risk tolerance, playbooks, drafting preferences,
     negotiation patterns, and internal precedents under access controls.
6. **Client-value delivery**
   - Faster and better legal work with traceability, not just more AI output.

## Required proof of work

Ask for:

- shipped workflow or tool;
- sample input and output;
- verification rubric;
- source provenance;
- known failure modes;
- client-value or internal-value metric.

Reject claims based only on enthusiasm, screenshots, tool lists, or generic
prompting.

## Guardrail

Do not confuse building a tool with building a business. Distribution, trust,
professional judgment, and client outcomes remain first-order constraints.
